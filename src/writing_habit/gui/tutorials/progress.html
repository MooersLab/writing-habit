Track project progress

The weekly comparison says how one week went. The Progress tab takes the long view. It draws one bar for each unfinished project, so you can see which projects are nearly done, which have barely started, and which deadlines are close. It also lets you say how much each project matters, and the coach uses those weights to suggest next week's plan.

The Progress tab with the practice week. The whole bar is the estimate, green is the time spent, and white is the time left. The days to each deadline follow the bar.

Make the progress table

In the window

  1. Open the week's file on the Schedule tab.
  2. Open the Progress tab and press Add projects from the current list. A table named progress is added at the end of the file, with a row for each numbered project of this semester's list. A project brings its deadline with it.
  3. Type the Est. hours each project is expected to need and its Weight. A scale from 0 to 1 works well. Type or correct the Deadline as YYYY-MM-DD.
  4. Press Update hours spent. The hours come from the zTimeSpent table of mytime.db, matched by the project's four-digit number. The tracker is only read, never written.
  5. Press Save to the week file. The table lives in the week file, so a new week's file made by copying this one carries it forward.

Read the chart

The projects are listed by number and name. A dark green piece past a tick mark is time spent beyond the estimate, which is a sign to raise the estimate or to ask why. The days to the deadline are amber within two weeks and red once the deadline has passed. A checked project whose weight is above 0.85 is highlighted in pale yellow. Hover over a bar for its numbers.

Plan next week from your progress

  1. On the Schedule tab choose Coach > Plan next week from progress.
  2. The week's planned hours are shared among the checked projects by weight, never more than a project has left, and each project's blocks are placed in the hours your energy profile rates best for new writing.
  3. Uncheck a project you will not work on next week and press Update the plan. The hours are shared again among the rest.
  4. Press Add the checked to Next week. The projects go on the Next week list, the suggested blocks are copied to the clipboard, and the boxes are remembered, so they start the same way next week.

The plan for next week, one line per project with its hours and blocks.

In Emacs

writing-habit-el reads and writes the same table. From the week's buffer:

M-x writing-habit-progress          the progress dashboard
M-x writing-habit-progress-update   fill Hours spent from mytime.db
M-x writing-habit-hours             the week as hours by day

In the dashboard, u updates the hours, a adds the current list's projects, e jumps to the table, and g redraws.
