What is new

A short tour of the recent additions, with the tutorial that covers each one.

The hour view

  • The blocks are drawn half transparent with outlines, so two blocks that share an hour, and the break between them, can both be seen.
  • Drag the top or bottom edge of a block to change its length, or drag its middle to move it to another time or day. Esc cancels. See Edit the schedule.
  • A block's further hours show only a down arrow, which keeps the grid quiet.

The weeks ahead

  • The Weeks row of buttons holds This week, eight lists from Next week to Eight weeks out, and the Grasscatcher. The Semesters row below holds the semester lists. Both rows wrap when the window is narrow.
  • Put a project in a later week to take it off this week's mind, and copy it into this week's legend when its week comes.

Progress and next week's plan

  • The Progress tab charts the hours spent on each unfinished project against its estimate, with the days to its deadline. See Track project progress.
  • Coach > Plan next week from progress shares next week's hours by your weights and places them in your best hours. Unchecked projects stay unchecked next week, and heavy checked ones are highlighted.

More from the coach

The Coach menu of the Schedule tab gained Rescue missed blocks, Ask about missed blocks, Number a new project, Triage carried projects, Monday message for my partner, and Semester review. The software makes every decision and computes every number, and a local model, when you turn it on, only words the note.

Safer with the time tracker

The Pomodoro count is written only to a table with the count's own columns, and never to zTimeSpent, so the tracker's table and its formulas stay as you made them. Every reading of mytime.db is read-only.

In Emacs

writing-habit-el now has the planning checks and helpers of the Coach menu, a progress dashboard, and an hour-by-day view of the week:

C-c C-; h   hour view
C-c C-; g   progress dashboard
C-c C-; U   update hours spent
C-c C-; A   add the current list's projects to the progress table
